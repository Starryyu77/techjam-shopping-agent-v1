# Evidence Tour Visual Refresh

## Direction

Turn the judge-facing Tour into a premium technical dossier: editorial hierarchy,
precise data typography, deep ink surfaces, and restrained cyan/lime evidence
signals. The memorable object is the evidence scorecard, not decorative chrome.

## Constraints

- Preserve the seven-step information architecture and all evidence IDs.
- Preserve 1440×900 recording mode and responsive behavior.
- Keep the site offline-capable: no remote fonts, images, or runtime services.
- Competition evidence remains blue/cyan; demo-only ads remain purple; unknown
  private-set performance remains amber.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keyboard focus states.

## Component treatment

- Hero: asymmetric editorial copy plus a high-contrast verified scorecard.
- Navigation: compact glass rail with a clear active-step signal.
- Cards: layered surfaces, subtle top highlights, stronger spacing hierarchy.
- Replay: scenario controls become a segmented instrument panel; override state
  transitions use color-coded summary cards without relying on color alone.
- Evaluation: tabular numbers, highlighted current row, compact metric tiles.
- Ads: visually distinct purple lab treatment, maintaining its demo-only label.

## Acceptance

- No evidence, route, or interaction regressions.
- Entire primary replay remains usable at 1440×900.
- Hero and replay remain readable at 1024px and narrow mobile widths.
- Full unit/evidence suite passes and browser console remains clean.

## Viewport-fit refinement

- At 1440×900 and 1366×768, every default tour step fits without page scrolling.
- Short desktop viewports hide the redundant brand bar during dense Replay steps,
  while retaining the progress rail and Competition Evidence label.
- Intent Override exposes only one internal scroll surface: the Top-10 list. Turn
  history and state are fully visible without clipping.
- Evaluation provenance and Closeout limitations use native disclosure controls,
  keeping low-frequency detail available without forcing it into the main frame.
- At 721–1024px, Turn and State use a two-column layout and Top-10 uses two columns.
  At phone widths, every panel returns to natural single-column document flow.
- Completed ad auctions compact their input cards and keep the winner, formula,
  sponsored slot, and organic-order invariant in the same frame.
