How the auction works: The winner is chosen by eCPM = bid × relevance (BM25 keyword match to the shopper's query), not by raw bid alone. Ads below a relevance floor are suppressed. Each impression spends the bid from the campaign budget; a campaign stops serving when it exhausts its budget. Organic ranking (the scored path) is never changed — promoted items appear in a separate labelled slot.
💡 How to try: Launch a campaign below, then open the Shopper view and type a query matching your keywords — watch your Promoted slot win the auction.
